About

Preserving What Matters Most

Time moves relentlessly forward, but a photograph holds the quiet power to stand still. Behind the lens, I capture genuine human connection — a proud smile, the radiant grace of an 18th debut, the shared triumph of an institution, and the unspoken warmth that binds people together.

Based in Cabuyao, Laguna, my focus is giving you a return ticket to the days that mattered most — transforming fleeting moments into enduring memories.
Andrei C. Capoy Photographer · EYSI CLICKS
